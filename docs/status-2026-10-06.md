## Current conclusion

EcoQoS remains a private, background-idle experiment. Stock Discord remains the Track A reference for active use. Track B now evaluates whether replacing the Electron shell produces a meaningful full-tree reduction, but its first unauthenticated WebView2 baseline is not a win and is not comparable to logged-in stock Discord. The repository must stay private until a shell passes the same-workload comparison and the required functional checks.
